Vest Markets (Vest Capital)

Perpetuals prop (offshore, crypto-settled)

Not CME futures. Vest Capital is a prop program on Vest's own perpetuals exchange. Its Prop Terms say all accounts, including Instant Funded, are simulated. The operator, Vest Exchange Inc., is based in Panama, and payouts are in USDC.

US traders: Vest's exchange Terms of Service list the United States as a prohibited jurisdiction. The prop accounts appear to be available separately under the Prop Terms. Check eligibility for your country before you buy.

Source: official site https://www.vestmarkets.com/

Model: One-time purchase; simulated perpetual-futures prop program (crypto-settled, USDC). Not a CME futures firm.
Drawdown: Static max drawdown, never trails: 6% of capital on evaluations; on Instant equal to the fee (2% on $500, 4% otherwise). Optional daily loss limit 3% or 4% (or none) on 1-Step, always 3% on 2-Step, never on Instant; DLL resets 8:00 PM ET. Any breach closes the account permanently.
Consistency: None published
Payouts: Claim Profit any time, no cap on amount or frequency; 80% or 90% split (chosen at purchase) on evaluations, 95% on Instant; credited to your Vest Primary Account in 24h, then withdraw USDC to a wallet. Claims are irrevocable.

Note: No resets: a breach means buying a new account. 1-Step has 36 price configurations (Silver $5K, Gold $10K, Platinum $25K; 10% or 20% target; 3%/4%/no DLL; 80/20 or 90/10), from $25 to $450; the 10%/3%/80-20 preset is shown. 2-Step target is 10% then a further 5%. No activation fee is listed (docs: one-time purchase). The Prop Terms say all accounts, including Instant, are simulated, while the docs say 'Real Vest capital. Not simulated.' Refund window: 28 days unused per the Refund Policy and Prop Terms (the Vest Capital overview says 3 days). Operator: Vest Exchange Inc., Panama. The exchange Terms list the United States and UK as prohibited jurisdictions; the Prop Terms do not.
